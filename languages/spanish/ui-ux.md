# Spanish — UI / UX

Inverted punctuation is not optional decoration — it is orthography.

## Must

- Preserve accents and ñ; use ¿ ? and ¡ ! correctly in full sentences
- Choose es-ES vs LatAm (es-419 or country) for vocabulary (ordenador vs computadora) and document it
- Allow expansion vs English; avoid cramped buttons
- tú/usted consistency per product surface

## Avoid

- ASCII n for ñ in user-facing text
- Mixing Spain and Mexico terminology randomly

## Review prompts

1. Does the first viewport read correctly in LTR?
2. Is address/tone (formal vs informal) consistent?
3. Do strings fit without clipping after translation expansion?
